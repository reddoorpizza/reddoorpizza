## Long Description

Red Door Pizza is a wood-fired pizzeria located at 401 Warrenheip St, Buninyong VIC 3357, approximately 11km (12 minutes' drive) from the Ballarat CBD.

Our menu features authentic wood-fired pizzas made with imported Italian fior di latte, slow-cooked meats sourced from the Buninyong Butcher, and fresh produce from Peaches Fruit Market. We offer gluten-free pizza bases, gluten-free pasta, vegetarian options, homemade pastas, starters, and desserts including locally made il piccolo gelato.

We are family-friendly with a dedicated kids corner featuring a play kitchen, books, and colouring pages, plus a specialised Kids/Teen menu.

We accommodate group bookings, work break-ups, Christmas parties, and private functions for up to 100 guests. Our venue includes cosy indoor dining, a relaxed lounge area, and a spacious outdoor beer garden with festoon lighting.

Our vacuum-sealed, take-home pizza bases are also available at select local stockists across the Ballarat region.
