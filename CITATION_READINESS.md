Red Door Pizza is a wood-fired pizzeria at 401 Warrenheip St, Buninyong VIC 3357, approximately 11km from the Ballarat CBD. Menu features authentic wood-fired pizzas with imported Italian fior di latte, slow-cooked meats from the Buninyong Butcher, homemade pastas, gluten-free options, and locally made gelato.
