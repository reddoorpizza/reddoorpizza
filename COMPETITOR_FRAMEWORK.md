## Competitor Research Checklist

For each competitor, investigate:

### Website
- [ ] Do they have a working website?
- [ ] Is the menu visible with prices?
- [ ] Do they offer online ordering?
- [ ] Is there a booking system?
- [ ] Do they have location-specific pages?
- [ ] Is there a blog or guide content?
- [ ] Do they use structured data (schema)?
- [ ] Is the site mobile-friendly?
- [ ] Do they have a sitemap?
- [ ] Are canonical tags present?

### Content
- [ ] Do they mention gluten-free options?
- [ ] Do they mention family/kids facilities?
- [ ] Do they mention functions/groups?
- [ ] Do they mention local ingredients?
- [ ] Do they have FAQ content?
- [ ] Do they mention Ballarat proximity?

### Local Signals
- [ ] Google Business Profile complete?
- [ ] Consistent NAP across directories?
- [ ] Reviews on Google?
- [ ] Reviews on other platforms?
- [ ] Local directory listings?

### Social/External
- [ ] Active Instagram?
- [ ] Active Facebook?
- [ ] Listed on food delivery platforms?
- [ ] Press mentions?

## Differentiation Priorities

Based on what competitors cannot easily copy:

### Tier 1 — Difficult to Copy
- Physical Buninyong location with local supplier relationships
- Wood-fired cooking method (requires oven investment)
- Kids corner with play kitchen (physical space)
- Take-home product at local IGAs (retail relationship)

### Tier 2 — Requires Investment
- Gluten-free pizza + pasta options
- Functions capacity for 100 guests
- Detailed guide content
- Strong technical SEO

### Tier 3 — Can Be Copied
- Menu items and descriptions
- Opening hours display
- Contact information
- Social media presence
