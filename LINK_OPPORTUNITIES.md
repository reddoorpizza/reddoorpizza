# Local Authority & Link Opportunities

This document identifies legitimate external authority opportunities for Red Door Pizza.

---

## Supplier Relationships

These are existing business relationships that could naturally lead to mentions/links:

### Buninyong Butcher
- **Relationship**: Red Door sources slow-cooked meats
- **Opportunity**: Supplier directory listing, mutual mention
- **Action**: Ask if they list local businesses they supply

### Peaches Fruit Market
- **Relationship**: Red Door sources fresh produce
- **Opportunity**: Supplier directory listing, mutual mention
- **Action**: Ask if they list local businesses they supply

### Il Piccolo Gelato
- **Relationship**: Red Door serves their gelato
- **Opportunity**: Stockist/retailer listing, mutual mention
- **Action**: Ask if they list venues that serve their product

### Local IGAs (Buninyong FoodWorks, Beaufort IGA, Creswick IGA, Elaine Farmgate)
- **Relationship**: Stock Red Door take-home pizza bases
- **Opportunity**: Retailer directory listing
- **Action**: Ask if they list local products they carry

## What NOT to Do

- Do not buy links
- Do not create fake reviews
- Do not spam directories
- Do not create PBNs or link farms
- Do not contact journalists with unsolicited pitches
- Do not create pages solely for link building
